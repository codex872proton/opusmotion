# SENIOR BOWL — sources and claim check

A 69-second vertical film (1080×1920, 60 fps) about the Lockheed D-21 / Project TAGBOARD–SENIOR BOWL
reconnaissance-drone flights over China, 1969–1971.

## Primary and supporting sources

1. **NRO, "D-21 Declassified: NRO Technology Ahead of its Time Finds a New Home"** (21 Mar 2019).
   <https://www.nro.gov/news-media-featured-stories/news-media-archive/News-Article/Article/1819032/d-21-declassified-nro-technology-ahead-of-its-time-finds-a-new-home/>
   — "speeds over Mach 3.3 at an altitude of over 90,000 feet"; "the release of 97 recently declassified NRO
   documents about the drone program"; "the D-21 program was cancelled in 1971".
2. **NRO, Declassified D-21 Drones Program Records** (document index).
   <https://www.nro.gov/foia-home/foia-declassified-nro-programs-and-projects/Declassified-D-21-Drones-Program-Records/>
3. **Roadrunners Internationale, "Project Tagboard/Senior Bowl Overview"** (written by a former Honeywell INS
   engineer, with Russ Buyse and Stan Moeschl).
   <https://www.roadrunnersinternationale.com/coldwarstories/Project_Tagboard_story_revised.pdf>
   — descent to "about 60,000 feet", explosive bolts eject the hatch "containing a special Hycon camera, its film,
   flight control computers and the Honeywell Inertial Navigation System"; retrieval by a "specially modified JC-130B";
   ships as backup; "Once the hatch was ejected, the drone would self-destruct."
4. **Wikipedia, "Lockheed D-21"** (secondary; used for mission chronology and museum details).
   <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_D-21>
5. **Wikipedia, "Black Cat Squadron"** — five ROC-flown U-2s shot down over the PRC, 1962–1967.
   <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Cat_Squadron>
6. **Wikipedia, "596 (nuclear test)"** — China's first nuclear test, 16 Oct 1964, Lop Nur, 40°48′45″N 89°47′24″E.
   <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/596_(nuclear_test)>
7. **Wikipedia, "China Aviation Museum"** — 中国航空博物馆, Beijing.
   <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Aviation_Museum>

## Artistic licence (deliberate, labelled or schematic)

- Aircraft are stylised silhouettes; the 3D D-21 is a parametric model built from published dimensions, not a scan.
- Scale and spacing in the launch and recovery diagrams are compressed for legibility.
- The wreck in the museum shot is an illustration, not a depiction of the actual exhibit layout.
- Terrain is real elevation data (zoom-6 DEM) with Natural Earth borders; relief is vertically exaggerated.
